A few things to check before booking

Seats and luggage

Travelling light? A smaller car should be fine for a couple of bags. For a bigger group, check the seats and luggage space before booking.

Automatic or manual

An automatic can be more relaxed in Karachi traffic. A manual usually costs a little less per day.

Petrol or diesel

Most of the cars here use petrol. The Fortuner is diesel, so keep that in mind when comparing running costs.
