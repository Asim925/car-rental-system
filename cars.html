before you choose a car

a few things you might want to keep in mind.

how many people are travelling?

if you're travelling with a few people, check the seating capacity before choosing. also think about luggage space, especially if you're going on a longer trip.

manual or automatic?

if you usually drive in karachi traffic, an automatic might be more comfortable. manual cars are also available if that's what you prefer.

don't forget the fuel type

most cars in our collection run on petrol, while the fortuner uses diesel. it's worth checking this before making your choice.
