Before you choose

Seats and luggage

Economy cars suit one to two bags. Choose an SUV when you travel with four or more people and luggage.

Automatic or manual

Automatic cars are easier in heavy traffic. Manual cars are usually cheaper per day.

Petrol or diesel

Most cars run on petrol. The Fortuner runs on diesel, which suits long highway distances.
