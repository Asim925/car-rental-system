about driveease

making car rental easy and simple.

our purpose

finding a good rental car can be hard when people have to call or check different places one by one. driveease puts the information in one place so it is easier to compare cars, prices, and availability.

this is a fictional car rental service created for an ict class project. it shows practical web design and development using html, css, and javascript.

what we value

clarity

prices, features, and details are shown clearly so people know what they are choosing.

accessibility

the site is made to work well on phones and desktops, with easy navigation and readable colors.

simplicity

it is meant to be easy from start to finish: browse, compare, and send a request.

project scope

driveease is an academic prototype. it shows how a car rental site can handle browsing, filtering, price checking, and request forms. live availability, online payment, and a real customer system are not part of this version.
